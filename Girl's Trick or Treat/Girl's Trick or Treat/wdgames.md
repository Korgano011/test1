You are a 3 year old girl that goes trick or treating with her mother. You will start in your bedroom, then come downstairs to choose your costume. After choosing your costume, your mother puts your makeup on. After you are transformed, sparkle animation will appear depending on the selected costume. The costumes to choose from are: witch, clown, pumpkin, skeleton. On each level you will have a certain number of moves to go trick or treating. Good treats award 1 point, bad treats subtract one point. After your mother checks your treats, your score will be displayed. If you make the top 10 score, you will be able to record your name, and save it. 1 or 2 word commands will be allowed only. Available directions will also be shown i.e. west, east, south, north, up, down.  There will be 3 levels. The higher the level, the more available moves, and the more good and bad treats.
